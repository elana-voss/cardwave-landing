features_h = Features
f_chat = <strong>Chat.</strong> Talk with one character or several, who take turns or carry on among themselves. Go back and try another path at any message.
f_cards = <strong>Cards.</strong> Make a character from scratch or in a conversation with the AI, and edit it on its own page.
f_library = <strong>Library.</strong> Find a character by name, tag or what it is about. The AI tags and summarises your cards.
f_memory = <strong>Memory.</strong> Characters remember what happened and can draw on documents you give them.
f_companion = <strong>Companion.</strong> A 3D figure on your desktop that talks with you, reads its replies aloud and keeps you company, also as an everyday assistant.
f_voices = <strong>Voices.</strong> Replies read aloud in each character's own voice, on your computer. Dictate instead of typing.
f_pictures = <strong>Pictures.</strong> Make an image inside a chat.
f_tools = <strong>Tools.</strong> Let the model search the web, work in one folder or run commands, each only as you allow.
f_coding = <strong>Coding team.</strong> Give it a goal and a folder, and a team of AI agents works on it for you.
f_local = <strong>Local models.</strong> Run models on your own computer, with no key and no account.
f_tabs = <strong>Tabs and themes.</strong> Keep several chats and cards open in tabs. Light, dark or one of five more themes.
f_locked = <strong>Locked and backed up.</strong> A password locks the app; a backup is one file.
f_devices = <strong>Phones and other computers.</strong> Open Cardwave in a browser on a phone or another computer in your home network. Early, not yet thoroughly tested.
f_language = <strong>Your language.</strong> Cardwave speaks twelve languages.
privacy_h = Privacy
privacy_p = Cardwave keeps cards, chats, keys and settings on your computer, in an encrypted database. No sign-up, no sync, no analytics in the app. It goes online only for what you ask of it: a message to the provider whose API key you entered, a download you start (the search model, a voice), the web tools you switch on, and a look on GitHub for a new version, which Settings can switch off.
byo_h = Bring your own AI
byo_p1 = Bring the AI you already use. Paste an API key and Cardwave knows whose it is: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, xAI, Mistral, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, NanoGPT and <a href="{providers}">more than 150 others</a>. Any OpenAI-compatible server works too, by its address.
byo_p2 = Or run models on your own computer: point Cardwave at a folder of GGUF files and it runs them itself, on your graphics card where it can.
