All features of Cardwave

Everything Cardwave does, by topic. The home page has the short version.

Chat

  • Talk with one character or a whole group.
  • In a group, characters take turns or talk among themselves.
  • Go back to any message and try another path.
  • Write a reply again, and flip between its versions.
  • Personas say who you are. Lorebooks say what the world holds.
  • Set the reply length and a prompt preset for each chat.
  • See what a reply drew on: lorebook entries and web pages.
  • Reply statistics: the model, its speed and the tokens sent.
  • Bubbles or plain text, as you like to read.

Characters

  • A character's page is also its editor. Click a text to change it.
  • Every text keeps its earlier versions.
  • AI tools improve a text, fix its spelling or change it as you ask.
  • The character generator makes a card from a few picks.
  • Or make a character together with the AI, in a conversation.
  • Translate a card into another language, side by side.
  • Make a picture for a card. Earlier pictures are kept.
  • Expressions and a 3D figure for each character.
  • Modes give a character other texts for another kind of chat.
  • Import SillyTavern PNG and CCV4 cards. Export either.

Library

  • Find a character by name, tag or meaning.
  • The AI sums up and tags each card.
  • Views: list, gallery, discovery, and posters.
  • By need: pick what you need, and see which characters offer it.
  • Favourites, an archive, and a trash with undo.
  • Lorebooks and personas in lists of their own.

Memory and documents

  • Characters remember what happened, every 20 messages.
  • Memories belong to a character and your persona.
  • See, change or delete each memory.
  • Give a chat or a character documents: text, Markdown, PDF or Word.

Voices and the companion

  • Replies read aloud in each character's own voice, on your computer.
  • Dictate instead of typing.
  • A 3D figure on your desktop, with a small chat beside it.
  • It shows feelings and moves with its mood. Pick it up and throw it.
  • Give a character a figure of its own: VRM, GLB or FBX.
  • As an everyday assistant, with tools and its own memory.

AI tools

  • Web search, the files of one folder, and commands, each as you allow.
  • The AI writes small tools for itself. They run in a sandbox.
  • Make a picture inside a chat.
  • Coding team: give it a goal and a folder, and AI agents work on it.

Models

  • More than 160 providers, known by their API key.
  • Any OpenAI-compatible server, by its address.
  • GGUF models run on your own computer, on the graphics card.
  • The search model finds cards, lorebook entries and documents by meaning.

Your data

  • Everything stays on your computer, in an encrypted database.
  • A password locks the app.
  • A backup is one file. Restore it even at the first start.
  • No sign-up, no sync, no analytics.

Tabs, themes and devices

  • Tabs keep several chats and cards open.
  • Light, dark and five more themes.
  • Twelve languages.
  • Open Cardwave on phones and other computers at home. Still early.
  • From anywhere too, with Tailscale.
  • An account for each person, with chats and characters of its own.
